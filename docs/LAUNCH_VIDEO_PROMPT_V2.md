# Kavach launch video, version 2 (paste this into Gemini to redo the video)

Redo the Kavach launch video. Keep the same look, music and honest small print, but make it 8 scenes (about 64 seconds)
so it shows everything Kavach does. Show each feature as a clear visual story, with big simple on-screen labels.

## The 8 scenes
1. **The trap (0-8 s).** Papa, in his late 60s, answers an unknown number. A pushy voice says he is under "digital arrest",
   must stay on the line and tell no one. Label: "A scam call".
2. **Kavach is listening (8-16 s).** Papa's phone shows the Kavach app: a live transcript of the call and a scam-risk
   meter climbing. Chips light up one by one: Authority, Threat, Secrecy, Payment. Label: "Kavach listens to every call".
   Normal calls stay quiet: show a second quick example, a call from a doctor, risk stays low, nothing happens. Label: "Normal calls: it stays silent".
3. **The warning in a trusted voice (16-24 s).** The phone glows teal. A calm familiar voice (the son's cloned voice)
   whispers to Papa only: "Papa, this is a scam. Don't pay." Show the scammer's mic cut off from Papa: the scammer cannot
   hear the warning. Label: "Warns Papa in a voice he trusts. The scammer hears nothing."
4. **Papa chooses (24-32 s).** A big red card floats over Papa's screen: "This looks like a scam. Let Kavach take over."
   Papa, scared, taps it. Label: "Scared? One tap: Kavach takes over."
5. **Kavach talks in Papa's voice (32-44 s).** Split screen. Left: the scammer, still talking, thinking he is speaking to
   Papa. Right: the Kavach screen shows Kavach replying in Papa's own cloned voice, slow and a little confused like a real
   elderly man, asking the scammer to repeat his name, badge number, bank account and UPI ID. An evidence list fills in
   live: Name, Phone number, UPI ID, Bank account, with the exact quote beside each. Label: "Kavach keeps the scammer
   talking, in Papa's voice, and collects the evidence. The scammer never knows."
6. **The son steps in (44-54 s).** The son, at a cafe, gets a Telegram alert on his phone with the message "Possible scam
   call detected" and a red button "End the call". He taps it. Back at Papa's house the call line drops and the scammer is
   gone. Label: "The son can end the call from his phone."
7. **A voice note from the son (54-60 s).** The son records a short voice message in Telegram. In Papa's living room, the
   voice of the son plays out of Papa's phone: "Papa, I'm coming, you did well." Show Papa smiling and the Kavach app
   banner "A message from your family is playing". Label: "The son's voice note plays on Papa's phone, even after the call is cut."
8. **The result and end card (60-68 s).** Family hug. Then a tidy screen: a PDF titled "1930 complaint draft (review
   before filing)" and the numbers "18 of 18 scam calls warned, 0 of 18 normal calls flagged". End card: "Kavach. A shield
   for the people we love." and "Built with AssemblyAI text-to-speech and speech-to-text". Small print on every frame
   that shows a number: "36 synthetic test calls, not real phone calls. Early prototype."

## Voice-over (about 60 s, one calm male voice)
"The scammer says: stay on the line, tell no one.
Kavach is already listening. Normal calls it leaves alone. But when the words turn to threats, secrecy and money, it
speaks to Papa, privately, in a voice he trusts. The scammer hears nothing.
If Papa is scared, one tap lets Kavach take over, in Papa's own voice, so the scammer keeps talking and Kavach collects the
name, the number, the bank account, the UPI ID, as evidence.
His son gets the alert at once. He can end the call from his phone. And he can send a voice note that plays on Papa's phone.
Then Kavach drafts the 1930 complaint, for a person to check and file.
On a held-out test of 36 calls: all 18 scams warned, none of the 18 normal calls flagged. Kavach. A shield for the people we love."

## Keep it honest
- It is a prototype and the test calls were synthetic. It works on calls that go through the Kavach app.
- The cloned voices belong to Papa and the son, who agreed in the app.
- No real agency logos, names or phone numbers. The scammer is only a voice and a silhouette.
