# Kavach launch video: prompts for Gemini (Veo)

Veo makes about 8 seconds per clip, so this is 6 clips (about 48 s) to join in any editor, plus one voice-over.
Format: 16:9, 1080p, works on X, LinkedIn and Reddit. Paste the master prompt first, then each clip prompt one by one.

## Master prompt (paste first)
Create a short, warm, realistic cinematic video series for "Kavach", an app that protects elderly parents from scam phone calls.
Look: natural light, shallow depth of field, calm teal and warm amber colour grade, modern Indian middle-class home, no
text inside the video frames (I add text later). People: Papa, a kind Indian man in his late 60s, and his adult son in his
30s. Rules: no real police, CBI or bank logos or badges (use a plain generic uniform or none), no real phone numbers,
no gore, no violence. The scammer is only a voice and a silhouette on a phone screen. Keep the mood tense but not scary,
then relieved and hopeful. Smooth natural motion, no flicker, consistent faces and clothes across all clips.

## Clip 1 (0-8 s): the trap
Evening in a living room. Papa, sitting in an armchair, answers his ringing phone and listens. His face goes from
polite to worried. The phone screen shows only an unknown number. Close-up of his hand gripping the phone. Slow push-in.
Audio: a muffled, pushy voice from the phone, no clear words.

## Clip 2 (8-16 s): the pressure
Same room. Papa looks around nervously, lowers his voice, glances at the family photo on the wall. Shot over his shoulder:
his thumb hovers over the screen. Clock on the wall shows time passing. Sense of being alone and cornered.

## Clip 3 (16-24 s): Kavach wakes up
Papa's phone rests on the table on speaker, with a calm teal glow starting at its screen edge. The glow pulses once and a
soft ring of light spreads. Papa's expression changes: he looks surprised and listens, as if hearing a familiar,
reassuring voice nearby. Gentle close-up on his eyes. Warm, safe feeling.

## Clip 4 (24-32 s): the son knows
Across the city, the son sits at a cafe table. His phone buzzes. He looks at the screen, a worried look, and taps it
quickly. Then he lifts the phone to record a short voice message, speaking calmly and kindly. Cut back: Papa, in his
armchair, smiles with relief.

## Clip 5 (32-40 s): the scam fails
Papa calmly puts the phone down, the call has ended. The son has now arrived at the door and they hug. A mother or sister
brings tea. Warm window light, sense of family and safety. The phone on the table shows a soft green glow.

## Clip 6 (40-48 s): the end card background
A slow, wide, peaceful shot of the living room at night, a lamp on, the family photo, Papa's phone on the table with a soft
teal glow. Leave open space on the left for text. Slow pull-back.

## Voice-over (one calm male voice, about 45 s, add it in the editor or ask Gemini for it)
"Scam calls pressure the people we love into acting before they think.
Kavach listens to the call, and the moment it sounds like a scam, it speaks to Papa, in the voice of someone he trusts.
It can take the call over for him, talk to the scammer and collect evidence, and tell his son straight away.
The son can cut the call, or send a voice note that plays on Papa's phone.
And Kavach prepares the complaint for 1930, ready for a human to check and file.
In our held-out test of 36 calls, it warned on all 18 scam calls, and on none of the 18 normal ones. Kavach. A shield for the people we love."

## Rules for the posts (please keep these true)
- Say "prototype" and "test calls were synthetic". Do not say it works on all phone calls: it works on calls that go
  through the Kavach app, because phones do not let apps hear normal network calls.
- Use the cloned voice only of someone who agreed. Papa's and the son's agreement is recorded in the app.
- Do not use a real person's or a real agency's name, logo or number in the video.
