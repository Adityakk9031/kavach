SCAM CALL SCRIPT FOR YOUR FRIEND (for a rehearsal with a real phone call)

Who this is for: the friend who plays the scammer on a normal phone call to you.
This is a made-up script. Nothing here is real: the officer, the cell, the UPI ID and the numbers are all fake.
Both of you must agree to the call being listened to by the laptop. Do not use anyone's real details.

HOW TO PLAY IT
- Read each line slowly and clearly, like a pushy officer. Pause 2 to 3 seconds after each paragraph, because
  Kavach decides what to do when you stop talking.
- Do not rush through the whole thing: the warning should arrive in the YOU-side headphones after paragraph 2 or 3.
- If you are interrupted, just carry on from the same paragraph.

PARAGRAPH 1 (opening)
"Hello, am I speaking with Mister Kumar? This is Inspector Vikram Rathore from the Mumbai Cyber Crime Cell. A parcel
booked in your name was seized at customs, with illegal drugs and fake passports inside."

PARAGRAPH 2 (fear and secrecy)
"Your Aadhaar card was used. This is now a criminal case. You must stay on this call and you must not tell anyone,
not even your family, or we will send officers to arrest you today."

PARAGRAPH 3 (urgency and payment)
"To clear your name you must pay a refundable verification deposit of fifty thousand rupees. Use the UPI ID
customs dot clear at okbank. Do it immediately, within one hour."

IF THE PERSON HANDS OVER TO KAVACH (the old confused man answers)
Keep going and answer his questions patiently. Give him these fake details when he asks:
  - Your name: Inspector Vikram Rathore. Cell: Mumbai Cyber Crime Cell. Badge number: four four seven one.
  - Bank account: five zero one zero zero two three four five six seven eight nine one, IFSC code H D F C zero zero
    zero one two three four.
  - Callback number: nine eight two zero one three three four five six.
  - Website: w w w dot r b i dash verify dot in.
He will ask you to repeat and to spell things. Do it. This is how Kavach collects the evidence.

END
"Mister Kumar, I am waiting. Pay now or the warrant goes out in one hour."
